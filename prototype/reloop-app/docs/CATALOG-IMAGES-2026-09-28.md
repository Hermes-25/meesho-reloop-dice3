# Generated catalogue photos

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, one image per product. The 12 original generated PNG files are copied into `public/assets/stock/` and are used only for illustrative demo stock. Real uploaded photographs are retained. Demo lot detail pages label the generated photos as examples, not inspection evidence.

Prompt set: realistic small-business wholesale stock photographs; one cohesive landscape 4:3 photo per item; warm grey tabletop, softly lit neutral stockroom, natural materials, clear product outline and breathing room for card crops; no text, brand names, logos, prices or people.

Subjects: tan crossbody handbags with minor surface scuffs; navy school backpacks; natural cotton tote bags; teal/rose/indigo cotton kurtas; printed cotton sarees; plain polo T-shirts; cotton cushion covers; terry bath towels; steel two-tier lunch boxes; ceramic mugs; canvas sneakers; silicone phone cases. Prompts specify unused stock except the crossbody bags. Photographs are examples of the product type and do not verify quantities or condition.
