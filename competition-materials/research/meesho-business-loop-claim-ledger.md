## Key inference

The software pillar alone is not defensible. The differentiated proposition is the closed loop: a cross-channel business system of record that learns demand, recommends verified sourcing, and converts sellable returns/overstock into graded pooled lots. Even then, damaged goods should not be promised back to the original wholesaler unless buyback terms were agreed at purchase. Recovery must route by condition to original suppliers, liquidators, refurbishers or authorised recyclers.

The data flywheel strengthens the product and creates future revenue, but it should be described as aggregate market intelligence rather than data resale. Individual seller economics, customer details and supplier relationships remain private. Consent for connected outside channels must be separate, clear and reversible; commercial insight products should use aggregation thresholds and prevent re-identification.
