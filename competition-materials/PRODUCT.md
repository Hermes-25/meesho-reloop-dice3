# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: a React-based Sites prototype, chosen for a coherent multi-role desktop flow, reusable interface components, and later hosted review.

## Users

- Meesho sellers recovering value from legitimately returned, damaged, excess, ageing, or obsolete stock.
- Verified wholesalers, liquidators, refurbishers, and manufacturers buying pooled recovery lots and later supplying fresh inventory.
- Meesho operations, trust, category, finance, and logistics teams monitoring marketplace health and exceptions.

## Product Purpose

ReLoop converts fragmented seller-side dead stock into graded, buyer-ready B2B lots. Sellers keep stock until a lot clears; verified buyers bid or buy; Valmo aggregates and moves the sold lot; the seller receives the winning bid and Meesho earns a separate buyer-side trust premium. After liquidity and price data mature, Meesho Source reuses the network for protected fresh-stock sourcing.

## Positioning

ReLoop is not another analytics dashboard or an RTO insurance product. It is a two-sided recovery market that combines Meesho's return signals, image-based verification, seller density, buyer matching, and Valmo movement to turn otherwise stranded stock back into cash.

## Operating Context

- Desktop-first prototype embedded in the logic of the Meesho Supplier Panel, with a separate role-appropriate wholesale view and an internal control tower.
- Seller flow: identify eligible stock, capture evidence, review grade/value, pool virtually, sell, schedule pickup, verify, receive payout.
- Buyer flow: verify business, discover lots, inspect evidence, bid, prepay, track aggregation, receive and raise bounded claims.
- Phase-two flow: sellers create or join fresh-stock RFQs; verified wholesalers quote and fulfil protected orders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Prototype data is illustrative and must not be presented as live Meesho data.
- A seller retains listed stock until sale and pickup; the system requires periodic quantity reconfirmation.
- Seller receives the cleared bid value without a deduction from Meesho B2C sales proceeds.
- Meesho's monetization is a separate buyer-side trust premium and later B2B sourcing fees.
- Wrong-return or fraud compensation eligibility is resolved before ReLoop; Meesho must not monetize an operational obligation.
- Pre-order identities are masked; post-order protection, GST documentation, claims, and Valmo tracking keep transactions on-platform.
- Desktop comes first; mobile adaptation follows after the desktop journey is approved.

## Brand Commitments

- Extend Meesho's current visual identity and familiar Supplier Panel interaction patterns.
- Use plain, action-led language suitable for sellers who may be new to complex digital workflows.
- Preserve Meesho's affordability and zero-commission seller promise.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-provided competition brief, interviews, working slides, past-winner references, solution documents, and financial model within the Round - 1 workspace.
- Public Meesho evidence supports image-based reverse-logistics verification, seller-panel navigation patterns, medium-density interfaces, coloured recognisable icons, and workflow simplification.
- No real production API, seller account, buyer account, or internal Meesho dataset is available; all prototype transactions and metrics are synthetic but plausible.

## Product Principles

1. Show the next money-relevant action before analytics.
2. Explain grade, price, fees, and variance before asking for commitment.
3. Keep inventory with the seller until demand is proven.
4. Separate normal claims and platform responsibility from voluntary recovery commerce.
5. Make every cross-party handoff traceable through evidence, status, and a recovery path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear status language, visible focus states, keyboard-operable controls, non-colour status cues, readable desktop typography, and upload guidance that remains understandable on low-bandwidth connections.
